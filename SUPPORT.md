# Support

Cornercase remote is the cornercase app for iPhone and Android. It follows, answers and starts the coding agents that cornercase runs on your computer (Linux or macOS). In the app, that computer is a machine.

## Getting help

To ask a question, report a problem or suggest something, open a [new issue](../../issues/new) in the app's repository. Issues are public, so never paste a pairing code or anything else you would not share.

## What to include

- **The app's version.** The App Store or TestFlight shows it on iPhone; on Android, the app's details in the phone's settings show it.
- **Your phone's system**: iOS or Android, and its version.
- **The version of cornercase** on the machine, and whether it runs Linux or macOS.
- **How the phone reaches the machine**: the same local network, or Tailscale.
- **What happened**: what you did, what you expected, and what the app did instead, with any message it showed.

## What the app needs

- **cornercase 0.13.78 or later** on the machine, with Settings › Remote › Phone app turned on.
- A phone on the same network as the machine, or both on your Tailscale network.
- iOS 15 or later on an iPhone, or Android 7.0 or later.

If the app cannot reach a machine, check these three things first.

## Privacy

The app collects nothing and talks only to the machines you pair it with. The [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) says what it keeps on your phone and how to delete it.
