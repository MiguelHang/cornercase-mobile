# Cornercase remote

Cornercase remote is the cornercase app for iPhone and Android. It follows, answers and starts the coding agents that cornercase runs on your computer (Linux or macOS), from your phone.

It talks only to the machines you pair it with, over your local network or Tailscale. It has no account, no server of its own and no analytics. Its source code is [on GitHub](../../../).
