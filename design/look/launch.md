# Launch and icon

A topic of [Look](../look.md): what shows before the first screen, and the app's icon. Moved out of `design/look.md` in the review of 2026-11-15, when that file passed the 40 KB of `DESIGN.md`.

## The launch

- **No white flash and a light status bar.** `tauri.conf.json` gives the window `backgroundColor` `#0b0c0e`: wry 0.57.1 (`wkwebview/mod.rs`, read on 2026-11-08) paints it on iOS on the WKWebView and on its view with `setOpaque(false)`; without it the view uses `systemBackgroundColor`, which is pure black in dark mode and white in light mode. `LaunchScreen.storyboard` takes the same colour in sRGB instead of `systemBackgroundColor`, and `project.yml` sets `UIUserInterfaceStyle: Dark`, so the status bar is light whatever the phone's setting. Checked in the iPhone 17 simulator, iOS 27.2, on 2026-11-08: the launch screen is the dark ground with a light status bar, and on a recording of the launch read frame by frame no frame between the home screen and the card is lighter than the ground.
- **The flash was `NoMachines`, not a colour** (055, reported from the user's iPhone: "for a moment it shows no machines connected, then it loads the saved one"). `App.svelte` mounted its router on `routeFor(idle, 0)`, which is `no-machines` while `machines` is empty, and painted it until `load()` read `machinesList`. Recorded on 2026-11-13 in the iPhone 18 Pro simulator, iOS 27.2, Xcode 27.2, with the embedded frontend of a debug build, the app terminated with `simctl terminate`, then `simctl io recordVideo` and `simctl launch`, read frame by frame:
  - With a saved machine that connects, in order: the storyboard's plain ground, then the WebView's, which look the same (about 4 s in all, a cold debug build); the wash of `body::before` alone for about 170 ms, as the bundle's CSS arrives before Svelte mounts; `NoMachines` whole for one frame (its mark and wordmark at the top and the card); `home` with `Connecting…` while the card fades out over 120 ms, its brand still on top and the board's mark pushed to the bottom; `home` alone with the board's pulsing mark near the top for about 200 ms; then the list of agents. So the mark appears in three places before the first useful screen.
  - With a saved machine whose bridge refuses, the same, with `Retrying in 1 s` in the bar from the first frame of `home` instead of `Connecting…`.
  - With no machine, the plain ground, the wash, then `NoMachines` in its final place in one frame: Svelte skips intro transitions on the first mount (`mount` without `intro: true`), so `riseIn` does not play on launch.
- **One launch layer in the page, gone when the app knows what to show.** `index.html` holds `#launch` outside `#app`: the ground with the mark of `Mark` at a unit of 8, flat, still and without its glow, styled inline so it paints with the page's first frame, before the bundle's CSS and before Svelte mounts. `App.svelte` paints no route until `machinesList` has answered, so `NoMachines` can no longer show for a saved machine, and `launch.ts` takes the layer away once the list is read and, with an active machine, its connection has left `connecting`; `connected` waits for the first list of agents too, since before it `home` shows the board's pulsing mark in another place. A `connect` call that answers with an error counts as ready too: it changes no state, so the bar would stay on `Connecting…` and the layer would wait for the cap with its error toast already under it. It leaves with the 120 ms `quadIn` fade of Motion, or at once with Reduce Motion, and is removed from the DOM. **A cap of 3 s** from when the bundle runs takes it away whatever the state, so a slow machine shows the normal screen with its bar instead of a layer that hangs. If the list itself has not answered by then, the cap leaves the plain ground, without a bar, until it does: painting `NoMachines` meanwhile would bring the flash back. The three commands of the load are synchronous in Rust, and on Android `machines_list` reads the Keystore in tens of milliseconds per route, so it is not expected to happen.
- **The storyboard is the plain ground, without the mark.** It first drew the mark too, with three views of fixed size centred on the whole view, and matched the layer's to the pixel, but the storyboard goes away as soon as the app's window shows, and the WebView paints its first frame 0.3 to 0.9 s later, showing only its background meanwhile: the mark went off and came back, a blink that is worse than a mark that appears once, a little later. Neither an `<img>` of the mark in SVG, which WebKit counts as content for its first paint, nor a bundle stylesheet that does not block rendering with the script at the end of the body shortened the gap (0.5 and 0.8 s), so it is the WebView starting and loading the page, not what the page holds. Rejected for 055: a native view over the WebView until its first paint, which is Rust and objc2 in `src-tauri`, a task of its own.
- **The mark sits at `50lvh` from the top, not centred in the layer.** In its first 2 to 4 frames WKWebView lays the page out and clips it at 778 of the 874 pt (the height without both safe areas), alternating with the full height, so a mark centred with `inset: 0` and a grid, or with `dvh`, jumped 48 pt; `lvh`, `vh` and `screen.height` kept their place in those frames. `lvh` because it says what it means and turns with the phone, while `screen.height` stays the portrait's in landscape. A `vh` declaration comes first as the fallback for Android WebViews older than Chrome 108 (`minSdk` 24), which do not know `lvh` and would otherwise drop the declaration and put the mark at the top.
- **Rejected**: taking the layer away after a fixed time, too long on a fast start and too short on a slow one, when the flash came from the order of the load and not its speed; the mark as an image in `Assets.xcassets`, a raster whose points need not match CSS pixels; `core-splashscreen` on Android (`design/architecture/android.md` › The launch); a glow or a pulse on the layer's mark, which the native screens cannot draw and which would put a second motion next to the board's pulse.
- **Checked live on 2026-11-13** in the iPhone 18 Pro simulator, iOS 27.2, Xcode 27.2, with the embedded frontend of a debug build, after uninstalling the app so no cached launch screen showed, recorded and read frame by frame as above (`simctl io recordVideo` writes only frames that change, so a 120 ms fade shows as 0 to 4 of them):
  - With a saved machine that connects: the plain ground (the storyboard, then the WebView's), then the layer's mark in one frame, centred at 603, 1,311 px of 1,206 × 2,622, still until it leaves, with no frame of `NoMachines` or of another ground; then the list of agents, under the fading layer in one of two recordings.
  - With no machine: `NoMachines` appears under the fading layer.
  - With the bridge refusing: the layer fades out as soon as the bar reads `Retrying in 1 s`; for those 55 ms its mark and the board's show together.
  - The cap, with a local proxy that lets the handshake through and holds every later frame 5 s: the layer left at 2.99 s over `Connecting…`. A port that never answers does not test it, since the transport's own connect timeout, also 3 s, turns the state to `retrying` first.
  - Unchecked: the user's iPhone, and Reduce Motion live (it is covered by the tests of `dismiss`).
- **Android draws the same mark before the WebView**: Android 12's splash shows it on the ground at 96 dp, centred in the window, and it matched the layer's to 1 px on 2026-11-13 in the emulator; below API 31 only the window's ground is dark (`design/architecture/android.md` › The launch).
- **`pnpm tauri ios dev` does not run xcodegen** once the project exists, so a change to `project.yml` reaches `cornercase-mobile_iOS/Info.plist` only by running `xcodegen generate --spec project.yml` in `src-tauri/gen/apple`; commit what follows from the change and drop the rest of the `.pbxproj` it rewrites. Each `ios dev` then writes `Info.plist` again without its final newline: that change is noise, revert it.

## App icon

- **The icon is the mark** of usecornercase.dev's favicon (`site/public/favicon.svg` in cornercase-terminal, a grid of 16 with 2 of margin) and of `Mark` (a grid of 12): in the mark's units `u`, the top bar `x 3–12, y 0–3`, the side bar `x 0–3, y 3–12` and the cursor `x 6–8, y 6–11`, the corner in `#005fff` and the cursor in `#eceef1`. It is drawn once, in SVG in `src-tauri/icons/`, as the favicon is: a `path` of `M…h…v…z` in whole pixels with `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`, centred by its box of 12 × 12 u. Every size for the desktop, iOS and Android comes out of those SVGs, so changing the mark is editing them and running one command.
- **Two SVGs, because the two systems mask differently.**
  - `icon.svg` is the mark on its ground, opaque, for the desktop, iOS and Android's classic icons (`ic_launcher.png` and `ic_launcher_round.png`, for API 24 and 25). The ground fills the whole square, with no rounded corners, which the system draws. `u = 48`, shifted 224 px: a mark of 576 px, 56 % of the side, whose farthest tip is about 407 px from the centre (39.8 %), inside the round icon's circle (50 %) and clear of iOS's rounded corner.
  - `icon-foreground.svg` is the mark alone on a transparent ground: the foreground of Android's adaptive icon, which the launcher crops from a canvas of 108 dp to the 72 dp it shows, with any mask inside the safe zone, a circle of 66 dp. `u = 32`, shifted 320 px: a mark of 384 px whose farthest tip is about 272 px from the centre (26.5 % of the canvas, under the 30.6 % of the safe zone), so it takes 40.5 of the 72 dp shown, the same 56 % as on iOS. Measured in the 432 px `mipmap-xxxhdpi/ic_launcher_foreground.png` that `tauri icon` writes: no pixel past 113.8 px from the centre, against 132 px for the safe zone, and the mark at 56.2 % of the visible side.
  - One SVG for both was rejected: it gives the mark one size, so at iOS's 56 % of the side it would reach past Android's safe zone and the mask would cut it, and at Android's it would take 37.5 % of the iOS icon's side.
- **`src-tauri/icons/icon.json` is `tauri icon`'s manifest**: the two SVGs (`default`, `android_fg`), `bg_color` and `android_fg_scale`. Its fields are described only by `pnpm tauri icon --help`, so they were measured: `android_fg_scale` 100 draws the foreground SVG at its own size (100 is no scaling, so the margins are the SVG's), and `bg_color` both fills iOS's PNGs and becomes the adaptive icon's background, `values/ic_launcher_background.xml`, so no `android_bg` is needed.
- **The ground is `#0c0e11`**, the favicon's, not the window's `--glass` `#0b0c0e` (Launch): the icon follows the other drawing of the mark, and the two are one step apart, which no eye tells on a home screen.
- **Flat, with no glow.** `Mark` lights its corner with a cobalt `box-shadow`; at 20 to 40 pt (Spotlight, Settings, notifications) a halo blurs the edges that make the mark, and the favicon is flat too.
- **Regenerating**: `pnpm tauri icon src-tauri/icons/icon.json` writes `src-tauri/icons/` (the desktop's and Windows' icons, which `bundle.icon` and `tauri-build` read), `gen/apple/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/` and `gen/android/app/src/main/res/` (`mipmap-*`, `mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml`, `values/ic_launcher_background.xml`), and everything it writes is in git, with no step by hand. A `pnpm` script for it was rejected: it runs once per change of the mark, and the command is already short.
  - **`src-tauri/icons/64x64.png` is in git** though nothing reads it (`bundle.icon` does not list it): the command writes it on every run, so deleting it would leave an untracked file, or a `rm` by hand, after each one. Rejected: deleting it and listing it in `.gitignore`, a rule for one file of 217 bytes.
  - **A second run changes only `icon.icns`**, with the same size and its blocks in another order: not a change of the icon, so it is not committed alone. `icon.ico` and every PNG come out byte for byte the same.
  - **It writes no `mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher_round.xml`**, and the manifest declares no `android:roundIcon`, so no reference is broken: a launcher masks `ic_launcher.xml` with its own shape.
- **Left out**: Android 13's themed icon (`android_monochrome`), since the theme tints the corner and the cursor alike and the mark's two tones are lost; iOS 18's dark and tinted variants and Icon Composer's `.icon`, since `tauri icon` 2.12.4 writes an `AppIcon.appiconset` of one appearance, on which iOS applies its own effects.
- **Checked live on 2026-11-10**, with Tauri CLI 2.12.4:
  - iPhone Air simulator, iOS 27.2 (24B8117), Xcode 27.2, after uninstalling the app so no cached icon showed: the home screen, Spotlight, Settings at 29 pt and the app switcher show the mark, its cursor still legible at 29 pt, so `u` stays 48.
  - Emulator `cornercase_api37`, Android 17 (API 37), Pixel Launcher, 1,080 × 2,400 at 420 dpi: the app drawer shows the whole mark inside the round mask (the corner of its box about 80 % of the mask's radius from the centre), and the icon of Recents, about 24 dp, stays legible. The debug build compiles without the template's drawables (`design/architecture/android.md`).
  - Unchecked: a real iPhone (the user's, pending), an API 24 or 25 device with the classic icons, and launchers with other masks, which the safe zone covers.
