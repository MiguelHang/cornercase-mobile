# Seen from the phone

A topic of [Chat](../chat.md): opening a `done` agent marks it reviewed, only `done`, where it is watched, a failure, no `--boot`, the fitted terminal.

- **Opening a `done` agent reviews it**: while the agent's screen or its terminal (passive or fitted) is on top and the page shows, a row in `done` sends `mark --pane P --reviewed` through the ordered queue (`mark_reviewed` in `src-tauri/src/commands/chat.rs`, `crates/core/src/cli/mark.rs`), once per arrival at `done`: on opening, on coming back to the foreground, and when the row turns `done` in sight. Upstream clears the `✓` without a view and without touching sizes, so the board drops its `✓` (out of Done by sections, its status in the tree) by the `status` event the `Store` already follows, with no state of its own (Board › Sections). Asked for in 029: opening is reviewing, with no button; whoever wants a reminder has `pending`.
- **Only `done`**: `--reviewed` also clears `pending`, the mark the user sets by hand, and `pending` wins over `done` in `status`, so a row in any other status sends nothing. A Mac that marks `pending` between the phone reading `done` and the `mark` arriving loses it (Upstream gaps).
- **Where it is watched**: `App.svelte`, not the agent's screen, since with its terminal on top the screen is not mounted. The `Chat` lives on under changes, a diff, files and Machines (Chat › The screen), so the watch follows `chatOf` of the top route, which is the chat only while an `agent` or `terminal` is on top, with `whileShown(document, …)` as the screen's other follows. `Chat.watchDone` keeps "marked for this arrival" as the row's `since`, which changes with every change of status (Board), not by time, so opening and the event at once send one, and a new arrival at `done` whose step in between the phone missed (a lost `working`, a reconnection, a whole `status` again) is marked too. A `since` that is filled in (the session showed `done` before the `status` event, Board) or lost (`Dropped`, a reopened stream) is the same arrival: only one time replacing another known time marks again, since a second `mark` of one arrival would go down the queue behind the first and widen the race with a `pending` set on the Mac. The cost: when a `mark` sent with no known `since` fails and the agent reaches `done` again with no status in between seen, that arrival is not tried again.
- **A failure** (no agent cornercase reads in the pane, a closed pane, the transport down, a reconnection that skips it) goes to the app's log, a line `cornercase-mobile: mark --pane P --reviewed failed: <reason>` on stderr written in Rust (the unified log on iOS, `logcat` on Android, as Connection › Network), without a toast; a `console.warn` in the WebView would only show with Web Inspector. It is tried again only on the next arrival at `done` or the next watch.
- **No `--boot`**: `mark --boot` exists since 0.13.30 with no feature naming it, and an older CLI refuses it, so the `mark` goes without, as `send` and `keys` (Upstream gaps › `--boot`).
- **Also with the fitted terminal**: its own view already counts as seen (Terminal › Fit to this phone › Seen while fitted), so the `mark` is redundant there, and harmless: it only goes for a row in `done`, never one in `pending`. Kept so that one watch covers the chat, the terminal with its switch off and fitted, rather than asking the terminal whether it is fitted.
- **Rejected**: the chat opening a sized `view` of its own to count as seen, which claims the tab and shrinks the Mac's PTY to the phone's width just to read the chat, though the terminal, fitted, does claim it (Terminal › Fitted by default); and doing nothing, which left the `✓` on the Mac and the row in Done after reading it on the phone.

## Checked live

- **Seen from the phone, on 2026-11-10** in the iPhone 17e simulator (iOS 27.2, Xcode 27.2, macOS 26.6.2, Tauri 2.12.5, the page embedded), against a bridge of its own on `127.0.0.1` and cornercase 0.13.19, with Claude Code 2.1.322 (Haiku 5.5) in two tabs of a project of its own, the taps made by XCUITest and the Mac's changes read from `cornercase events`. On the board both were subagents in the tree, under their parent in Idle: `Ready` with its `✓`, `Done` in its label, and `Second`, marked with `mark --pane` and blue. Opening `Ready` turned its pane `done → idle` on the Mac, and `status` stopped giving `done`; the header still read `Done` in the dump 3 s after the tap and `Idle` on the next look, and back on the board the row read `Idle`, without its `✓`. Opening `Second` left it `pending`, on the Mac and in the header.
- **In sight**: with the terminal on top, passive as it opened before 043, a prompt sent from the Mac turned the pane `working`, `done` and `idle` within the same second, and its tab took no `controller`. With the board on top, or the workspace's changes over the chat, it stayed `done` (15 s and more); `Back` to the chat turned it `idle`. With Settings over the app, seen in a screenshot, it stayed `done`, and bringing the app back turned it `idle` within 7 s of `simctl launch`; in a first try, with Settings launched cold 2 s before the prompt and its screen not checked, the `mark` went, the app still on top.
- **Not checked live**: the fitted terminal, whose own view sees the tab (Terminal › Fit to this phone › Seen while fitted); the Mac's window, whose `✓` follows the same status; a `mark` that fails; an iPhone.
