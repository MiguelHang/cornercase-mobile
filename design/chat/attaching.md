# Attaching an image

A topic of [Chat](../chat.md): the button and the picker, the images shrunk on the phone, kept until `Send` and bounded, sending and a paste that fails, the thumbnails.

- **The button**: `Attach image`, Lucide's `plus` in an icon button of 44 × 44 px, the first in the capsule (Chat › Answering › The capsule). It can be tapped while the screen has its row and the agent is not gone, also while it is `waiting` and while a text is sent, since the image only waits on the phone until the next send. While the picker is open and the image is read, it is busy, with the spinner in place of its icon, and takes no second tap; with 4 images waiting it is disabled (Kept on the phone). Its state lives in `src/lib/attach.svelte.ts` (`Attachments`, one per `Chat`), with its commands injected like the others.
- **The picker**: iOS's `PHPickerViewController`, images only, one at a time, from a plugin of our own (`src-tauri/plugins/photos`; Architecture › Plugins of our own, which also says why not `tauri-plugin-dialog`). It runs out of the app's process and needs no access to the photo library, so there is no permission to ask nor `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`. Cancelling it (its `Cancel`, or a swipe down) does nothing: no toast and no thumbnail.
- **Shrunk on the phone, by the system**: ImageIO makes a thumbnail (`CGImageSourceCreateThumbnailAtIndex`) whose longest side is at most 2,048 px, with its proportions; a smaller image keeps its size. The EXIF orientation is applied, and the image is encoded again from its pixels, so no metadata (the GPS position included) goes to the Mac. PNG when the source is PNG (screenshots, transparency), JPEG at quality 0.85 otherwise, HEIC included. ImageIO reads only what the thumbnail needs, not a 48 MP photo whole. The name that goes is the picker's suggested name with the extension of what was written (`IMG_0042.HEIC` becomes `IMG_0042.jpg`), or `pasted.png` or `pasted.jpg` without one (`crates/core/src/cli/paste_file.rs`). Only an image's extension is replaced (`png`, `jpg`, `jpeg`, `heic`, `heif`, `gif`, `webp`, `tif`, `tiff`, in any case): the picker often suggests a name without one, and a name with dots is kept whole (`Screenshot 2026-11-09 at 10.42.13` becomes `Screenshot 2026-11-09 at 10.42.13.png`, not `… at 10.42.png`).
- **And a small one to show**: from the same source, ImageIO makes a second thumbnail whose longest side is at most 192 px, JPEG at 0.7, a few KB, for the composer (The thumbnails). If it cannot be made, the image still goes, without it. Rejected: 018's chip of text with a `×` added, while the task asked for a thumbnail.
- **The bytes never reach the WebView**: Swift writes both images to the app's temporary folder and answers Rust with their paths, the name and the kind. The `attach(pane)` command (`src-tauri/src/commands/chat/attach.rs`) reads the files and removes them at once, also when one cannot be read. It refuses an empty image, and one over 32 MiB (upstream's `MAX_IMAGE_BYTES`, its own cap). The WebView gets the image's id, name and size and the small thumbnail as a `data:` URL (`thumbnail_url`; the CSP allows `img-src data:`), or `null` for a cancel; never the image.
- **Kept on the phone until `Send`** (041): attaching no longer pastes. The image waits in `AppState` (`src-tauri/src/state/attached.rs`) under its id and its pane, and goes to the Mac only with the next send (Sending). 018 pasted it as it was attached, so its path was in the agent's input before the user had decided to send it, and a path typed into the agent's input cannot be taken back (Upstream gaps). The user asked on 2026-11-10 to remove an image before sending, as Claude and WhatsApp let one do: kept on the phone, its `×` drops it, and the Mac never gets it. It also ends 018's chips that outlived the screen: an image left unsent leaves no path in the agent's input. The upload moves from attaching to sending: a JPEG of 2,048 px weighs about 1 MB, so over LTE a send with images shows `Sending…` for seconds. Accepted, as in Claude and WhatsApp. Rejected: pasting on attach with a `×` that only hides the chip, which sends the image anyway; pasting on attach with a `×` that sends `keys backspace`, a workaround that depends on how each agent shows the path (Claude Code turns it into `[Image #1]`) and on an input the chat cannot see.
  - **Bounded, since it is memory**: at most 4 images per pane (D5 of 041, a limit of memory nobody asked for), after which `+` is disabled, and 64 MiB over all the panes, since a PNG of 2,048 × 2,048 px can weigh 12 MB; past either, `attach` refuses with a toast.
  - **Released** when its `×` removes it (`discard(ids)`), once pasted, when the `Chat` goes (`Back`, the agent gone, another machine; `Chat.leave`, which `App` calls as it drops the `Chat`) and when the WebView starts loading a page (`lifecycle::on_page_load`): a WebView loaded again has lost the ids, and nothing else would release them.
- **Sending**: `Send` takes the images waiting and, under its one bubble, pastes each with `paste_attached(pane, id)`, one after the other in the order they were attached, then sends the text (Send forced with images). Each is `paste-file --pane P --name=<name> --json -` by `Queue::ask`, with the image as `Stdin::Bytes`, so it reaches the agent after the keys and sends tapped before it, as a command of its own (Connection › the ordered queue, Upstream gaps). `--name=` is glued to its value, so a name that starts with `-` is not read as an option. The answer `{pane, path, bytes}` is read leniently (`Pasted`: defaults, unknown keys ignored), and its fixture is cornercase 0.13.19's output. On the Mac, upstream keeps the file in a folder of its own and types `<path> ` into the agent's input, without Enter; it removes the file with its pane or after 7 days, so the app removes nothing there.
- **When a paste fails**, the text does not go: the bubble says `Not sent: <reason>`, with a send's reasons (Chat › Answering › What comes back) and the refusal of a CLI on the Mac older than its server that lacks `paste-file`. The images go back over the capsule, those pasted already marked `On Mac` (`pasted`), so `Retry` pastes only those still on the phone and sends with `--force`. A paste that lost its answer (`it may have been sent, but no answer came back`) counts as not pasted, as 036 counts such a text as not sent: the image stays on the phone and `Retry` pastes it again, so if the first one did reach the agent, its input gets the path twice. The `×` of an image on the Mac removes its thumbnail but not its path, which nothing can take back (Upstream gaps), so a toast says `The image stays in <agent>'s input on the Mac`, and the next send is forced too, until one succeeds. A server without `paste-file` cannot connect at all, since `REQUIRED` names it (Connection › The contract).
- **The thumbnails** (`src/routes/agent/Thumbnails.svelte`): each image waiting shows over the capsule as a square thumbnail of 56 px (Look › Components) with a `×`, `Remove <name>` for VoiceOver, in a row that scrolls sideways within itself. Its name for VoiceOver is the name that goes and its size in binary units (`IMG_0042.jpg, 1.5 MiB`, as the 32 MiB cap), with `, on the Mac` once pasted. Without its small thumbnail it is a blank square with the same name. They live with the `Chat`: under the terminal and Machines, and released when it goes (Kept on the phone). While an image is being attached `Send` rests (`Chat.canSend`), so a text does not leave without the image whose picker is open. The row's `Enter` presses Enter alone: the images are not on the Mac yet, so it no longer takes them, as 018's did.
- **`Send` forced with images**: upstream types a pasted path through what a person's paste goes through, so the pane counts as drafting and a plain `send` is held until that draft lapses, 60 s without input, while the chat's `--timeout 30` ends first in `nothing was sent` (Upstream gaps). So a send that pasted images, or that comes after one whose images stayed on the Mac, sends `send --pane P --force --enter --timeout 30 -`: the text joins the paths in the agent's input and all go in one turn. With the field empty and images waiting, `Send` is enabled too and ends with `keys --pane P enter`, which submits the images alone. `--force` skips upstream's holds and its refusal over a dialog or a poll, but still waits for another send's Enter. The chat keeps its own `waiting` rule, since `Send` rests while the agent asks, so an image attached while the agent waits leaves only with the next send, where 018 pasted it into whatever was there. Lost with it: an image for a question's `Type something.`, which the chat cannot type anyway (Chat › Left out); accepted. A bounded exception to Left out: the draft `--force` passes is the paths the same send just pasted. Rejected: `Send` plain with a tap on `Enter` asked for, which sends the image and the text as two turns; typing the text with `input` and then `keys enter`, which confirms no turn, so a text that did not go would show no toast.
- **What fails while attaching is a toast and leaves no thumbnail**: the picker's own refusals (no screen to show it on, already open, a photo that cannot be loaded, read, decoded or written, and off iOS `only the iOS app has a photo picker`), an empty image, one over 32 MiB, a fifth image of the pane and the 64 MiB over all of them. Removing an image whose `discard` fails is a toast too, and its thumbnail goes all the same.

## Checked live

- **Attaching an image, on 2026-11-09** in the iPhone 17 simulator (iOS 27.2, Xcode 27.2, macOS 26.6.2, Tauri 2.12.5), against the bridge on the LAN and cornercase 0.13.19, with Claude Code 2.1.321 (Haiku 5.5), the images added with `xcrun simctl addmedia` and the taps made by XCUITest. `Attach image` opened the system's picker over the whole screen, the button disabled under it; its `Cancel` left no chip, no toast and nothing in the agent's input. A PNG of 1,179 × 2,556 px reached the Mac as a PNG of 945 × 2,048, its chip `shot.png` `72 KB`; a HEIC of 5,712 × 4,284 as `photo.jpg`, a JPEG of 2,048 × 1,536, `104 KB`. Claude Code showed each pasted path as `[Image #1]` and `[Image #2]` in its input. A text typed with both chips there and sent went with them in one turn, and Claude told the text and colour of each image; the chips went, the field emptied and `Send` rested again. The sizes then read `KB`; the chip writes `KiB` since.
- **A long name and an image alone**: the chip of a name of 70 characters was cut with an ellipsis, its size still showing, on the 402 px screen. That path, long enough to wrap, stayed as text in Claude Code's input rather than an `[Image #3]`; `Send` with the field empty pressed Enter, and Claude opened the file with its Read tool and described it. Either way the agent read the image. Not checked live yet: the spinner, which the picker covers while it is open; the toasts of a failure; an iPhone, which needs a signing team.
