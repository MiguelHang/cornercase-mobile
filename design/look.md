# Look

Follows cornercase-gpui's `design/widgets.md` and `src/theme.rs`: usecornercase.dev's colours, glass on one ground, rounded shapes with air, cobalt, the mark, motion. The tokens are ported from `theme.rs` into CSS custom properties; what changes for touch and small screens is recorded here. gpui's sources were read on `main` on 2026-11-08.

Topics, in `design/look/`:

- [Navigation](look/navigation.md) (`src/lib/router.svelte.ts`, `src/lib/navigation.ts`, `src/lib/platform.ts`, `src/lib/backSwipe.ts`, `src/lib/edge.svelte.ts`, `src/lib/backButton.ts`): the router as state and its base, the ways in and back, the back button and its label, one `goBack`, the iOS edge swipe (thresholds, touch events, the shared edge, `translateX`, lifting), Android's variant and its system back, what was checked live and what was rejected.

## Tokens

- **`src/lib/theme/tokens.css` keeps `theme.rs`'s names in kebab-case** (`brand_light` is `--brand-light`, `radius::CARD` is `--radius-card`), so a colour is found by the same name in both repositories. The colours are `mod colours`; `--glass` is gpui's opaque ground, the one it uses on Linux.
- **The whole palette is ported, not only what the first screen uses.** The states, `--veil`, `--field`, `--selection` and the rest are one piece of data copied as a whole, so the two repositories cannot drift apart; that is not dead code, which is about functions and components. A test checks that every `var(--x)` in `src/` is defined, in `tokens.css` or in the same file (a component's own property, like `Mark`'s `--unit`); it does not check that every token is used.
- **Spacing, fonts and durations are not in `theme.rs`, so their names are ours.** `--gap` (8 px) and the panel margins `--inset` (12 px) and `--inset-wide` (16 px) come from the literals in gpui's `ui/mod.rs`; `--inset-card` (28 px) is a card's own padding, shared by `Emblem`'s page and the actions under it so that the two keep the card symmetric; `--enter`, `--leave` and `--pop` from `ui/motion.rs`'s `ENTER`, `LEAVE` and `POP`. The diff's tints are ours too, since `theme.rs` has none: `--diff-added` and `--diff-removed` are `--done` and `--failed` at 10 %, their `-strong` forms, for the words that changed, at 32 % (`design/changes-and-files.md` › The screens). The durations also live in `src/lib/ui/motion.ts`, which the transitions read, and a test keeps the two equal.
- **No Tokyo Night colour** in the app's own look: Tokyo Night is the terminal's palette only, kept with the terminal (`src/lib/terminal/style.ts`, Terminal › Colours and styles).
- Colours with an alpha are written `rgb(r g b / a)` from the token's channels, not `color-mix()`, which iOS 15 lacks (the deployment target is 15.0).

## Ground and glass

- **The ground is opaque.** In gpui the glass shows the desktop behind the window; behind the WebView there is nothing, so the page paints gpui's Linux ground, `--glass`, with `glass::wash` at the top: `--brand` at 12 % fading to nothing over the top 180 px. The wash is on a `position: fixed` layer, so it does not scroll away and the rubber band of iOS scrolling never shows a light gap.
- **Floating cards keep gpui's values**: the vertical gradient from `#1c1f24` at 88 % to `#101215` at 91 %, the 1 px border, the `lift` shadow and the highlight along the top. Unlike gpui, WebKit does blur the page's own content under a `backdrop-filter`, so `blur(24px)` is set with and without the `-webkit-` prefix (iOS 15–17 only read the prefixed one). On the empty screen only the ground and the wash sit behind the card, so the blur barely shows; it will matter once content scrolls under a card. Blur costs GPU on large scrolling surfaces, so it goes on floating cards only, never on lists or the ground.
- **Checked by eye in the iPhone 17 simulator, iOS 27.2, on 2026-11-08**: the card stands out from the ground at gpui's alphas, so none changed. The card's title is 20 px semibold, its lines 16 px with a line height of 1.4, the wordmark 22 px and the button's label 16 px medium. gpui sets 12–13 px text and a 17 px title for a mouse and a desktop; on a phone, body text goes to 15–17 px and titles to 20–22 px. The lines use `text-wrap: pretty`, which kept a single word from ending a paragraph; older WebKit ignores it. The mark and the card sit at the top of the screen rather than its middle, as the screens with machines will.

## Touch and motion

- **Every control is at least 44 px tall**, Apple's minimum touch target, and the pressed state is `:active`, never hover, which touch screens lack.
- **WebKit on iOS applies `:active` only to elements with a touch listener**, so `Button` and each toast carry an empty `ontouchstart`. Checked with a real touch on 2026-11-09 in the iPhone 17 simulator, iOS 27.2 (an XCUITest press, which goes through the simulator's touch events like a finger): while `Add machine` is held it is `--brand-hover` (`#0050d8` read in the screenshots), and it goes back on release. Held for 10 s it does not click, since WebKit takes it as a long press; a tap opens the form.
- **Three tones, `primary`, `plain` and `danger`**: primary is cobalt, plain is gpui's `filled` of `Tone::Plain` (a `--hover` fill, a `--border` edge and `--soft` text), pressed to `--selected` with an `--edge` border. A form pairs them: `Cancel` plain, the action primary. Danger came with its first use, deleting a machine (027): `--failed` text on `--failed` at 10 % with an edge at 35 %, pressed to 18 % and 70 % (`color-mix`, so it follows the token). It marks only what cannot be undone: `Delete machine` and its confirmation's `Delete`, and the chat's `Ctrl-C` once armed, whose next tap interrupts the agent (Chat › Keys).
- **A resting button** keeps the primary fill at 40 % opacity and is `disabled`, `aria-disabled` and deaf to touches, like gpui's `resting_button`.
- **A busy button** is gpui's `busy_button`: the spinner before a label that says what is going on (`Adding…`), `disabled`, `aria-busy` and deaf to touches, but at full opacity, since it is working and not unavailable. With Reduce Motion the spinner stands still and the label alone says that it works.
- **The spinner** (`Spinner.svelte`) is Lucide's `loader-circle` at 12 px turning once every 900 ms in CSS, gpui's `spin`. It is mounted only while something works, so it never turns at rest.
- **Motion follows `ui/motion.rs`**: entering rises 14 px and fades in over 180 ms with `cubicOut` (gpui's `ease_out`), leaving fades out over 120 ms with `quadIn` (its `ease_in`), both from `svelte/transition` and `svelte/easing`. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, every duration is 0. Checked on 2026-11-08 in the same simulator with Reduce Motion on and off, on a recording of the launch read frame by frame: with motion, the card passes through intermediate positions and brightness; with Reduce Motion, it appears in its final place in one frame. Nothing animates forever while idle.
- **`dropIn` enters from the top**: `riseIn` with the 14 px negated, for the toasts, which come down from the top edge; they leave with `fadeOut`.
- **The pulse is gpui's `motion::pulse`**: opacity from 1 to 0.3 and back over 1,400 ms (`--pulse`, which a test keeps equal to `PULSE_MS`), on the connection bar's dot and the cursor of the mark on `home`, only while connecting (Connection › The connection bar). It is a CSS animation, not a Svelte transition, since it loops, defined once in `base.css` as the global `.pulsing` class, which each component sets; with Reduce Motion it does not run. It and the working orbit (Components) are the only animations that last, and each ends with its state.
- **A chevron on a row's right edge means going to another screen**, as in iOS's lists, so what unfolds in place puts its disclosure on the left: the board's rows and the files' tree. The user took the board's old arrow on the right for the way to the chat (Board › Tree and folding). The chat's subagents line is the one left on the right (Board › Left out).
- **The board's disclosure turns**: its `chevron-right` rotates 90° over `ENTER_MS` as the branch unfolds and back as it folds, with a CSS transition's default `ease`, at once with Reduce Motion (a `--turn` of 0 ms, read once from `prefersReducedMotion`).
- **`.spoken` hides text from the eye and not from VoiceOver**, such as the board's `h1` and the connection bar's `Retrying` without its countdown: one global class in `base.css`, like `.pulsing`, rather than a copy in each component's style.

## Fonts

- **System fonts only**: `--font-ui` is `system-ui` (SF Pro on iOS) and `--font-mono` is `ui-monospace` (SF Mono). The website's Archivo and JetBrains Mono were rejected for now: they add weight to the bundle, bring their licences into the app, and the CSP allows only `'self'`, so they would have to be bundled rather than fetched.

## Icons

- **Lucide is copied, not installed**, as gpui does in `assets/icons/`: version 0.460.0, with its ISC `LICENSE` next to the shapes in `src/lib/ui/icons/`. A package would bring in thousands of icons for the few the app draws.
- **The SVG's shapes are a table in `icons/shapes.ts`**, drawn by `Icon.svelte` with `stroke="currentColor"` and a stroke width of 1.75. Rejected: the `.svg` file pasted into a component (its `<!-- @license -->` line is a markup comment, which `scripts/check-rules.mjs` refuses), imported raw into `{@html}` (an error under `eslint-plugin-svelte`'s recommended rules), an `<img>` (it does not inherit `currentColor`) and a CSS `-webkit-mask-image` (it depends on the prefix on iOS 15 and goes through the CSP's `img-src`). An icon is added to the table, with its shapes copied from the same Lucide version, when a screen first needs it.

## Toasts

- **Where they come from**: the `toast` event, and the UI's own failures: a command's `CommandError` and a rejected IPC promise, which the bindings throw rather than return (`Toasts.failed`). The UI's own `info` toasts say what an action did where no screen shows it: a started agent's `warning`, and a workspace created (`design/starting-work.md`).
- **The logic is pure** (`src/lib/toasts.ts`, with the clock passed in), and `toasts.svelte.ts` holds the stack in `$state` with one timer set for the next toast to expire, rather than one per toast.
- **Adapted from gpui's `app/toasts.rs`**:
  - At most 3 rather than 5, the oldest dropped when a fourth comes: a phone has room for fewer.
  - At the top, under the connection bar and inside the safe area, rather than at the bottom right. On a phone the bottom belongs to the keyboard and the home indicator, and iOS's own banners come from the top. They enter downward (`dropIn`).
  - Each lasts 4 s (`info`) or 8 s (`error`) from when it arrives.
  - A toast equal to one still showing (same level and text) does not stack: it merges into it, counts `×N` and starts its time again, like `push_toast`. A `Store` repeats a warning, such as one per line of a stream that does not parse (Store › Lines are read leniently). A failed connection attempt is not a toast: its error is the connection bar's second line, so a stopped bridge shows none.
  - A tap dismisses it: there is no hover to show a close button on. A button fills the whole card, at least 44 px tall; it sits inside the `Glass` and not around it, since a button holds only phrasing content and `Glass` is a `div`.
- **Each one is a `Glass` card** with gpui's 3 px bar, `--accent` for info and `--failed` for error, with its glow. The stack lets touches through between the cards, so it never blocks the screen under it, and it is a polite live region.
- **Checked on 2026-11-09** in the iPhone 17 simulator, iOS 27.2: three equal error toasts and a fourth, pushed by a temporary line in `App.svelte` through a hot reload (nothing in the app sends a toast on demand), showed as one `×3` card and the other, under the two-line bar of `retrying`. A tap on the first removed it and the second moved up.

## Forms

- **`Field`** (`src/lib/ui/Field.svelte`): its label above and its error's line under it (`Labelled`), a `--field` fill, an `--edge` border, `--radius-control` and at least 44 px. The text is 16 px: below that, iOS zooms the page when the field takes focus. On focus, an `--accent` border and gpui's `glass::ring`, 3 px of `--brand` at 32 % (`color-mix`, so it follows the token). The error's line keeps its height when empty, so an error does not move the buttons; with an error, the field is `aria-invalid` and described by it.
- **`TextArea`** (`src/lib/ui/TextArea.svelte`), beside `Field`, with the same fill, border, ring and 16 px, for the chat's answer: it starts at one line and grows with its text up to about six, then scrolls. It grows without script: a hidden copy of the text in the same grid cell sets its height (`data-value` and `::after`), so the height follows each keystroke without measuring. The copy clips what passes its maximum (`overflow: hidden`): hidden text still counts in the page's scroll area, and a long draft would hang below the field and lengthen the page with blank space. Return adds a line; the form's button sends (`design/chat.md` › Answering). `bare` (041) leaves out its border, fill, radius and ring, which the chat's capsule draws around it and its buttons (Components), and keeps the 16 px, the growth and the maximum. In the chat its label is `aria-label`, since the chat shows no label above it; given an `error` (a form's field, such as the start screen's prompt), it takes `Field`'s face through `Labelled`: the label above, the error's line under it, `aria-invalid` and described by it.
- **A field turns text selection back on**: `base.css` sets `user-select: none` on `body`, and without `user-select: text` (and its `-webkit-` form) iOS neither selects in the field nor offers Paste on a long press, which is how the bridge's URL arrives.
- **A URL field** has no autocapitalisation, autocorrection or spell check, and `inputmode="url"`, so a pasted URL stays as it was pasted.
- **The first machine's form is inline**, in the empty screen's card under the emblem, like the Add form of gpui's `design/machines.md`: `Machine code`, `Name`, then `Cancel` and `Add`. Rejected: a sheet rising from the bottom. In WKWebView the keyboard covers what is `position: fixed` at the bottom (the visual viewport shrinks, the layout viewport does not), so the sheet would have to follow `visualViewport`. `Sheet` comes with its first real use: neither 027 nor 023 needed it. The start screen, which the ticket wanted as a sheet, is a screen of its own pushed over the board, with its own scroll, since its prompt always opens the keyboard (`design/starting-work.md` › The screen). What the form checks and does: Connection › Adding a machine.

## Launch

- **No white flash and a light status bar.** `tauri.conf.json` gives the window `backgroundColor` `#0b0c0e`: wry 0.57.1 (`wkwebview/mod.rs`, read on 2026-11-08) paints it on iOS on the WKWebView and on its view with `setOpaque(false)`; without it the view uses `systemBackgroundColor`, which is pure black in dark mode and white in light mode. `LaunchScreen.storyboard` takes the same colour in sRGB instead of `systemBackgroundColor`, and `project.yml` sets `UIUserInterfaceStyle: Dark`, so the status bar is light whatever the phone's setting. - **`pnpm tauri ios dev` does not run xcodegen** once the project exists, so a change to `project.yml` reaches `cornercase-mobile_iOS/Info.plist` only by running `xcodegen generate --spec project.yml` in `src-tauri/gen/apple`; commit what follows from the change and drop the rest of the `.pbxproj` it rewrites. ## App icon

- **The icon is the mark** of usecornercase.dev's favicon (`site/public/favicon.svg` in cornercase-terminal, a grid of 16 with 2 of margin) and of `Mark` (a grid of 12): in the mark's units `u`, the top bar `x 3–12, y 0–3`, the side bar `x 0–3, y 3–12` and the cursor `x 6–8, y 6–11`, the corner in `#005fff` and the cursor in `#eceef1`. It is drawn once, in SVG in `src-tauri/icons/`, as the favicon is: a `path` of `M…h…v…z` in whole pixels with `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`, centred by its box of 12 × 12 u. Every size for the desktop, iOS and Android comes out of those SVGs, so changing the mark is editing them and running one command.
- **Two SVGs, because the two systems mask differently.**
  - `icon.svg` is the mark on its ground, opaque, for the desktop, iOS and Android's classic icons (`ic_launcher.png` and `ic_launcher_round.png`, for API 24 and 25). The ground fills the whole square, with no rounded corners, which the system draws. `u = 48`, shifted 224 px: a mark of 576 px, 56 % of the side, whose farthest tip is about 407 px from the centre (39.8 %), inside the round icon's circle (50 %) and clear of iOS's rounded corner.
  - `icon-foreground.svg` is the mark alone on a transparent ground: the foreground of Android's adaptive icon, which the launcher crops from a canvas of 108 dp to the 72 dp it shows, with any mask inside the safe zone, a circle of 66 dp. `u = 32`, shifted 320 px: a mark of 384 px whose farthest tip is about 272 px from the centre (26.5 % of the canvas, under the 30.6 % of the safe zone), so it takes 40.5 of the 72 dp shown, the same 56 % as on iOS. Measured in the 432 px `mipmap-xxxhdpi/ic_launcher_foreground.png` that `tauri icon` writes: no pixel past 113.8 px from the centre, against 132 px for the safe zone, and the mark at 56.2 % of the visible side.
  - One SVG for both was rejected: it gives the mark one size, so at iOS's 56 % of the side it would reach past Android's safe zone and the mask would cut it, and at Android's it would take 37.5 % of the iOS icon's side.
- **`src-tauri/icons/icon.json` is `tauri icon`'s manifest**: the two SVGs (`default`, `android_fg`), `bg_color` and `android_fg_scale`. Its fields are described only by `pnpm tauri icon --help`, so they were measured: `android_fg_scale` 100 draws the foreground SVG at its own size (100 is no scaling, so the margins are the SVG's), and `bg_color` both fills iOS's PNGs and becomes the adaptive icon's background, `values/ic_launcher_background.xml`, so no `android_bg` is needed.
- **The ground is `#0c0e11`**, the favicon's, not the window's `--glass` `#0b0c0e` (Launch): the icon follows the other drawing of the mark, and the two are one step apart, which no eye tells on a home screen.
- **Flat, with no glow.** `Mark` lights its corner with a cobalt `box-shadow`; at 20 to 40 pt (Spotlight, Settings, notifications) a halo blurs the edges that make the mark, and the favicon is flat too.
- **Regenerating**: `pnpm tauri icon src-tauri/icons/icon.json` writes `src-tauri/icons/` (the desktop's and Windows' icons, which `bundle.icon` and `tauri-build` read), `gen/apple/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/` and `gen/android/app/src/main/res/` (`mipmap-*`, `mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml`, `values/ic_launcher_background.xml`), and everything it writes is in git, with no step by hand. A `pnpm` script for it was rejected: it runs once per change of the mark, and the command is already short.
  - **`src-tauri/icons/64x64.png` is in git** though nothing reads it (`bundle.icon` does not list it): the command writes it on every run, so deleting it would leave an untracked file, or a `rm` by hand, after each one. Rejected: deleting it and listing it in `.gitignore`, a rule for one file of 217 bytes.
  - **A second run changes only `icon.icns`**, with the same size and its blocks in another order: not a change of the icon, so it is not committed alone. `icon.ico` and every PNG come out byte for byte the same.
  - **It writes no `mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher_round.xml`**, and the manifest declares no `android:roundIcon`, so no reference is broken: a launcher masks `ic_launcher.xml` with its own shape.
- **Left out**: Android 13's themed icon (`android_monochrome`), since the theme tints the corner and the cursor alike and the mark's two tones are lost; iOS 18's dark and tinted variants and Icon Composer's `.icon`, since `tauri icon` 2.12.4 writes an `AppIcon.appiconset` of one appearance, on which iOS applies its own effects.
- **Checked live on 2026-11-10**, with Tauri CLI 2.12.4:
  - iPhone Air simulator, iOS 27.2 (24B8117), Xcode 27.2, after uninstalling the app so no cached icon showed: the home screen, Spotlight, Settings at 29 pt and the app switcher show the mark, its cursor still legible at 29 pt, so `u` stays 48.
  - Emulator `cornercase_api37`, Android 17 (API 37), Pixel Launcher, 1,080 × 2,400 at 420 dpi: the app drawer shows the whole mark inside the round mask (the corner of its box about 80 % of the mask's radius from the centre), and the icon of Recents, about 24 dp, stays legible. The debug build compiles without the template's drawables (`design/architecture/android.md`).
  - Unchecked: a real iPhone (the user's, pending), an API 24 or 25 device with the classic icons, and launchers with other masks, which the safe zone covers.
