# Chat

The agent's screen a row of the board opens (`src/routes/agent/Agent.svelte`, Board › A row): its header, which stays at the top (The screen), a thread of the conversation as the phone saw it, your sends and the agent's messages as markdown, kept on the phone (Kept on the phone), and a live line of what the agent does (The thread), and a composer to answer it (`src/routes/agent/Composer.svelte`): a rounded capsule with the field for text (Answering) and an icon that opens a row of keys over it to answer with a key (Keys). Its state lives in `src/lib/chat.svelte.ts` (`Chat`), without markup, so vitest tests it; the commands come in by injection, as in `addMachine.svelte.ts`. The thread's rules are pure functions in `src/lib/thread.ts`, with `now` as an argument, as `board.ts` does, and the conversation's in `src/lib/history.ts`. The capsule's `+` attaches images from the phone's photos, which wait on the phone and reach the agent's input with the next send (Attaching an image).

Topics, in `design/chat/`:

- [Answering](chat/answering.md) (`src/routes/agent/Composer.svelte`, `src/lib/chat.svelte.ts`): the capsule and its field, a send through the queue, what comes back on your bubble, `--timeout 30`, a false `not confirmed`, why `waiting` takes no text.

## Markdown

- **A tree, never HTML**: `src/lib/markdown.ts` parses the text with `mdast-util-from-markdown` and GFM (Architecture › Dependencies) and keeps a closed tree of its own: paragraphs, headings, emphasis, strong, strikethrough, inline code, code blocks, lists (with their checkboxes), quotes, GFM tables, breaks and rules. `Markdown.svelte` and `MarkdownNode.svelte` draw it with Svelte's own elements, so nothing goes through `{@html}`, which `svelte/no-at-html-tags` refuses and no comment may silence.
- **Headings one level down**: `#` is an `h2`, `##` an `h3`, and so on up to `h6`, since the agent's title is the `h1` of its screen (Look › Components) and VoiceOver walks a screen by its headings. They all look the same (`.heading`), so only the outline changes.
- **What is not drawn as itself**: raw HTML stays as its text, a link as its words, an image as its `alt`, and a node the tree does not know as its text. An `<a>` in the WebView would take the user out of the app; the CSP already blocks images from elsewhere, but no `<img>` is drawn either. Links that open are left out.
- **Code blocks and tables scroll sideways** inside their own container, the code in `--font-mono`, so the screen itself never does. The text is parsed in the WebView at each read and not virtualised: a message of tens of KB is accepted as it is.

## Gone

- **The agent is gone** when a list of rows lacks its pane (the pane was closed, or the agent quit and left its shell), or when the rows turn `null` after the screen had seen its row (a restart, a new `Store`), or when the active machine is not the one it was opened on (The screen). Then it says `This agent is no longer running.` under the bar and its back button, the field goes, and the text typed is lost; the conversation stays on the phone (Kept on the phone).
- **For good**: a later list with the same pane does not bring it back. After a restart the server may give that id to another agent, and the screen must neither show that agent's text nor send it what was typed for the first one (Upstream gaps › `--boot`).
- **Before the first list** (`null` without having seen the row: connecting, a new `Store`) the screen does not know yet and shows the board's mark (Board › A row).
- **An agent the app just started** has no row yet: `Chat.see` takes `starting` from its route, and until the row comes a list without the pane does not count it as gone, while the screen says `Starting <agent>…` (Starting work › After it starts). Once the row was seen, or on another machine, the rules above hold.
