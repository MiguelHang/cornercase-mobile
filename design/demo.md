# Demo

Code: `crates/core/src/demo.rs`, `crates/core/src/demo/` (its data in `demo/data/*.json`), `crates/core/src/cli/contract/served.rs`, `src-tauri/src/commands/demo.rs`, the `Demo` of `src-tauri/src/state.rs`, `src/lib/ui/DemoTag.svelte`, `src/lib/ui/DemoOffer.svelte`, `src/lib/demoExit.ts`, and `Try a demo` and `Leave demo` in `src/App.svelte`.

A Demo Mac the phone simulates on its own, so the app can be tried without a Mac running cornercase (064): App Review (guideline 2.1) has to get past the first screen, the store's screenshots need a board with agents on it, and anyone curious can see what the app does before installing cornercase. It needs no network, writes nothing, and is marked as a demo wherever it shows.

## What it is

- **A transport, not a mock of the app** (`DemoTransport`, `crates/core/src/demo.rs`): it implements `Transport` and answers the command lines the app already sends, so every screen runs its real code (`Store`, the queue, the terminal's grid, the question's card, changes and files) over it, and no screen of the session (board, chat, terminal, changes, files) branches on the demo; only the bar, Machines and the machine's `Edit` tell it apart (below). The tests of `demo/` read it through those same consumers on a paused clock, never by matching its JSON.
- **A world with state** (`demo/world.rs`), behind an `Arc<Mutex<_>>` that every command of the demo shares: the session's projects, workspaces, tabs and panes, each pane's status and since when, the replies, the questions, each terminal's rows, the files and their diffs, the viewed marks and the ids it hands out. A command changes it and bumps its revision, and the `--follow` streams wake on that revision through a `watch` and print their next line.
- **Dispatched by subcommand and flags, not by the exact argv** (`demo/dispatch.rs`): a new flag or a reordering on the app's side keeps working. What it does not know ends with code 2 and `error: the demo has no answer for …`, as a CLI that refuses a subcommand does, so a screen shows its usual error rather than hanging.
- **What it serves**: `contract`; the `Store`'s three streams (`status --follow`, `status --app --follow`, `events`); the queue's `requests --follow` and `input --follow --json`; the chat's `read --last-message`, `send`, `keys`, `paste-file` and `mark`; the terminal's `view` and `surface` (fitted and passive, a row wider than the screen wrapped onto the next as a terminal does, never cut); `changes` (the list as a stream, a file's diff, the viewed mark); `files` (list, read, search); `account list`, `start` and `close`; and `issue sources`, `issue list` and `flow list`, answered empty.
- **The contract is built from `REQUIRED` and `SINCE`** (`cli/contract/served.rs`), not kept as a fixed JSON: every operation, feature and stream the app requires, available, with the newest version `SINCE` names and the boot `demo`. When `REQUIRED` grows, the demo grows with it and never shows `missing`.
- **The sample**: two fictional projects under `/Users/demo/src/`, `acme-store`, an online shop with a worktree, and `weather-kit`, a Swift library, with agents working, one waiting on a question, one done and one idle, and subagents under them so the board shows its tree. The board's since times are minutes before the moment the demo was entered (`ages.json`), so it always looks recent. The terminals print in colour, the workspaces have changed files whose diffs agree with the files Files shows, and `account list` offers three agents and a few accounts.
- **It lives**: a message sent makes the agent work for 2 to 4 s of `tokio::time`, then rest with a reply other than the one before (the replies rotate per pane and turn), whose `written` moves only when that turn ends, so neither going to work nor being marked reviewed reads as a new message in the chat; an agent the demo started has not written yet, and `read --last-message` refuses it as cornercase does (`Error: the claude agent in pane 19 has not written a message yet`) until its first turn ends; choosing an option of the question resolves it, and that agent works and ends `done`; a started agent gets a new row, in a new worktree when one is asked for, works and ends `done` with its prompt as its title; closing an agent removes its tabs and those of the agents under it; marking a `done` agent reviewed makes it idle.

## Decisions

- **The Demo Mac lives only in memory** (D1): it is not in `machines.json`, has no secret in the Keychain or the Keystore, and its chat's history stays in `AppState`'s `Demo`, emptied on leaving, instead of the cache folder. That leaves no trace by construction, not by cleaning up. Rejected: saving it with a `Route::Demo`, which would change the file's format and add a variant to a `Route` that pairing, probing, merging and the routes all match as `cli` only. Its cost: the demo is lost when the system ends the app (below).
- **A new transport for production, not `FakeTransport`** (D2): `FakeTransport` answers an exact argv with canned lines, lives behind the core's `test-util`, and cannot reply to a message or show an agent that was just started, whose ids and paths are made as it runs. `DemoTransport` shares `emit` with it (`transport.rs`), and `FakeTransport` stays for tests only (`connection.md`).
- **One reserved id** (D3): `u32::MAX`, `DEMO` in `state.rs`, which the registry never hands out and which fits the bindings' `u32`. The UI gets it from the bindings as `DEMO_MACHINE` (`architecture.md`), to show the board connecting to it before it exists. Core's `demo::machine` gives it a stand-in `cli` route, `demo:0`, that nothing dials: the demo's `Launcher` ignores the route and hands every connection the same `DemoTransport`.
- **One demo at a time, and it is the active machine** (D4): `demo_start` remembers the machine that was active and launches the demo's `Store` in its place, without touching the registry's `active`. Connecting to another machine leaves the demo, and the UI lists the machines again once it has joined, so the Demo Mac's row goes and `Try a demo` comes back (`DemoExit.switched`); `Try a demo` while in it does nothing. Rejected: the demo as a machine beside the active one, with probes of its own, which would need a second `Store` the app has nowhere to keep.
- **Leaving** (`demo_leave`, or removing the Demo Mac from Machines with the usual confirmation): the demo's `Store` stops, the bar's connection is told `none`, and the machine that was active comes back when it is still in the registry. The UI then goes back to the board and reloads as it does at launch (`load`), both from the bar and after the deletion (`DemoExit`, `src/lib/demoExit.ts`): it shows the machine that came back, already connecting, or the first screen when none is left. Rejected: the usual steps after deleting a machine, which keep the connection the UI had, here the demo's `none`, and leave the board empty while the backend is on the machine that came back.
- **The queue speaks `requests --follow` and `input --follow` for real** (D5): every send, key and mark comes back confirmed. Rejected: answering `unexpected argument '--follow'` to push the queue onto its one-shot fallback, a degraded path the screenshots would then show.
- **Time is `tokio::time`** (D6), so the tests pause and advance it; the world keeps the deadlines and settles them whenever a command or a stream looks at it.
- **Off the network, the disk and the foreground** (`src-tauri`): its `Store` never starts `Offline`, network changes and the return to the foreground leave it alone (`network.rs`, `lifecycle.rs`), so the bar never says `No network` or `Retrying`; `forward` neither remembers it nor asks it for routes (`events.rs`); probing it in Machines answers at once with no transport (`probe.rs`); and `history_load` and `history_save` keep its chat in memory (`commands/history.rs`).
- **It is offered as a line of text, not a button** (068): `No Mac nearby? Try a demo` (`DemoOffer`, `look.md`) under the empty screen's card and under `Add machine` in Machines. In 064 it was a second, plain button under `Add machine`; tried on an iPhone, it competed with the primary, which is what a real user needs, while the demo is for someone without a Mac, App Review among them, who still finds it on the first screen. Rejected: keeping it in the card as a quiet button, still read as a second action.
- **It shows as the demo**: `machines_list` adds the Demo Mac, `demo: true`, last and active, while the demo is on; its connection carries an empty route, so the bar and its row name it with no `via` and never an address; `DemoTag` marks it on the bar in every state and in its row (`look.md`); its `Edit` holds only `Delete machine` (`connection/machines.md`); `Leave demo` sits on the bar only in the demo; and `Try a demo` sits under `Add machine` on the first screen and in Machines, gone while the demo is on (`connection/machines.md`).
- **Fictional, in English**: every name, path, branch, account and message in the demo's code and data is made up, under a `demo` home. A test (`demo/tests/privacy.rs`) reads every file of `src/demo` and fails on the name, e-mail and home of whoever runs it (from `git config user.name`, `user.email` and `$HOME`, so the repo itself names no one; a company is caught only through its mail's domain), on `@gmail`, an address under another home, an e-mail outside the reserved `example.*` domains, a public IPv4 address or anything shaped like a token. It compares whole units, never their pieces: the name, the mail's user and the home's user, each when it has two words or more, in every spelling (`jane hartwell`, `janehartwell`, `jane.hartwell`, `jane_hartwell`, `jane-hartwell`), and the mail's domain without its last label (`globex-labs`, four letters or more, none for `example.*`), each found only as a whole word. A first name or a surname alone, or an identity of one word, could be a word of the demo itself (`mark --reviewed`, `page`, the `demo` home), so the result would depend on who runs it; the homes and the mails are still caught by their shape. The company is the one unit of a single word, as there is no other way to catch it: a company whose name the demo already uses, such as `page.io`, fails the test for whoever works there. Rejected: listing the developer's and the company's names in the test, which would publish the very names it keeps out; comparing against hashes of them, which hides them but still pins the test to one person; and comparing every word of four letters or more of the identity, which failed for a Mark or a Will.

## Limits

- **No issues or flows**: Start › Issue says no tracker is connected and the flows' list is empty, with no error. Sample issues and flows are left for later.
- **No echo in the terminal**: typing is accepted and confirmed (`{"events": 1}` per event), but the screen does not change.
- **Lost when the system ends the app**: the next launch opens the machine that was active before the demo, or the first screen, since the demo was never saved.
- **An attached image is dropped**: the photo picker is the real one, and the image is shrunk and sent through `paste-file` as usual, but the demo only counts its bytes and answers a made-up path, so it never leaves the phone.
- **No failures**: the demo does not simulate a lost connection, `missing`, `not_paired`, retries or the network.
- **The working agents of the sample stay working** until they are sent a message; only the agents the demo sets going end on their own.
- **A message does not answer the open question**: sent to the agent that asks, it works and rests idle as after any message, while the question stays open in its terminal and its card until an option is chosen. Taking the text as the answer would need the demo to read `send`'s text, which it ignores today, for a path the options already cover.
- **The replies are not about the task**: six made-up replies rotate per pane and turn, so an agent fixing the cart may answer about an importer.

## Checked live

- **On 2026-11-17** in the iPhone 17 simulator (iOS 27.2, Xcode 27.2, Tauri 2.12.5), the app from `tauri ios build --debug` with `dist/` embedded, installed afresh, with no machine, the taps made by XCUITest. `Try a demo` under `Add machine` opened the board on `Demo Mac` with `DEMO` and no address: `Fix rounding in the cart totals` under Waiting, `Move the forecast cache to Swift 6` under Done and `Add Apple Pay to the checkout` under Working with `+2`, which unfolded into its two subagents.
  - **The question**: the waiting agent's chat showed its card, `Rounding` and three options; `Round the total` turned it working and then done, and the terminal showed `✔ Rounding: Round the total`.
  - **A message**: sent to the finished agent, it showed `Sent` at once, then `claude is working…`, and a reply other than the one before 2 s later, under the bubble. A photo from the system's picker went with another message, shown as its chip, `Sent`.
  - **Terminal, Changes, Files**: the terminals in colour, fitted and passive; Changes listed five files with their counts, opened `PaymentForm.tsx`'s diff and took a viewed mark; Files listed the tree, opened `README.md` and found three files for `pay`.
  - **Start**: Agent offered the three workspaces, `claude` and `personal`; a prompt in `weather-kit › main` made a new row that worked and finished with a reply, titled with the prompt; Issue said `No issue tracker is connected`. Closing that agent, after its confirmation, took it off the board; opening a done agent turned it idle.
  - **Leaving**: deleting the Demo Mac from Machines, and `Leave demo` on the bar after entering again, each went back to the first screen with `Try a demo`; the app's container held no `machines.json` and no history folder afterwards. Machines, during the demo, listed the Demo Mac with its tag and no `Try a demo`.
  - **What it changed** (T18): the chat showed every reply twice, and the old one again as `More to come…` once the agent worked, since `written` followed each change of status, now its own time; the terminal cut its rows at the phone's width, now wrapped; an agent just started showed a canned reply as its message, now refused as cornercase does; and `Rename` on the Demo Mac answered `no saved machine has that id`, now gone with its routes.
- Not checked: `Retrying` and `No network` never showing with the network off (CA7), covered by `src-tauri`'s tests of `network.rs` and `lifecycle.rs`; going back to a machine that was active before the demo, which needs a paired Mac, covered by `src-tauri`'s tests of `demo_leave`.
