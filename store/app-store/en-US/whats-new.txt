The first release: follow, answer and start cornercase's coding agents from your iPhone. It needs cornercase 0.13.78 or later on your computer (Linux or macOS).

- Pair with your computer: scan the QR code of cornercase's Settings > Remote > Phone app, or paste the code. Add one machine per computer and switch between them.
- See every agent on one board, with its status, the ones that need you first, and the agents they started folded under them. It updates live.
- Talk to an agent: read its conversation, answer it, tap the options of its questions, send keys, and attach images from your photos.
- Open the live terminal, fitted to your phone's width, and type into it.
- Start new work: an agent with a prompt, in an open workspace or a new worktree, or an agent or a flow on an issue.
- Check the changes and the files: read each diff and mark it viewed, browse the workspace's files and search them by name.
- Close an agent: swipe a row left, or tap Close agent on its screen.
- Try it without a machine: tap Try a demo on the first screen to open a simulated demo machine with sample agents.
